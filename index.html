Guide

How to read the chance

Each night gets one number that combines four things. All four must line up:

  • Activity – is the aurora strong enough to reach where the ship is? Up in the Arctic (Tromsø, Alta) it is overhead on most nights, even when "quiet". Further south it needs a geomagnetic storm.
  • Clear sky – the chance of at least a 2-hour gap with ≤40% cloud in the dark. In October this is usually the deciding factor on the Norwegian coast.
  • Darkness – sun at least 12° below the horizon.
  • Moon & lights – moonlight and town lights wash out faint aurora. The moon is new on 10 Oct, so this trip is well timed.

Ratings are conservative: ▲ GOOD ≥40% · ◆ FAIR 25–40% · ▼ LOW 10–25% · ✕ POOR <10%.

Confidence tells you how far ahead we are looking. Weeks ahead only the trend is known; aurora itself can only be predicted reliably 30–90 minutes in advance.

The numbers the experts watch

  • Kp (0–9) – global geomagnetic activity. Kp 1–2 is enough in Tromsø/Alta, Kp 3+ around Trondheim/Ålesund, Kp 6+ over the southern North Sea.
  • Bz – direction of the solar wind's magnetic field. Negative (south) for 20+ minutes = the door is open; the stronger and longer, the better.
  • Solar wind speed – above ~500 km/s helps.
  • OVATION – NOAA's 30–90 minute aurora model: the probability of aurora overhead at a given spot.
  • 27-day recurrence – the Sun turns every ~27 days, so what happened 27 days before a night often repeats.
  • CME – a solar eruption; if one is heading to Earth, NASA's model gives an arrival time and expected Kp.

Viewing tips on the ship

  • Go to the darkest open deck, away from the lights, and face north.
  • Give your eyes 10–15 minutes to adjust, and don't look at your phone screen in between.
  • A weak aurora can look like a greyish-white cloud to the eye. Your phone's night mode (3–10 s, phone braced against the railing) shows the green.
  • At sea you have the darkest sky; in port the sea-facing side is best.
  • Ask guest services whether the bridge announces aurora sightings or offers a wake-up call.

Alerts on your iPhone

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store (free, no account needed).
  2. Tap +, then subscribe to the topic name you were given (it is private, so don't share it).
  3. Allow notifications. For night-time alerts: Settings → Focus → Sleep → Apps → add ntfy, and turn on Time Sensitive Notifications.

During the cruise you get an evening outlook around 17:00 ship time, and a "go outside" alert when live activity is good, the sky over the ship is clear and it is dark. Conservative thresholds, max one alert per 90 minutes unless it gets stronger.
